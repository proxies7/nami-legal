Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · applies to the Nami Accounting application and its use of Google APIs.

Nami Accounting ("the application") is an internal bookkeeping automation operated by Athikit Supap ("the operator", "we"). This policy explains exactly how the application accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes Google user data. It applies to the Google account the operator authorises; the application is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the operator's own organisation.

1. Google user data we access

The application requests only these OAuth scopes, and only for the authorised account:

ScopeWhat it allowsWhat we actually read or write
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive Full Drive access Files inside the designated finance folders only: newly uploaded bills, invoices, delivery notes and payment slips. The application lists folder contents, downloads document files, renames them and moves them between folders of the same account.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets Full Sheets access One ledger spreadsheet owned by the operator: it appends rows to the Transactions and Items tabs and reads them back for validation and reporting.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents Full Docs access Creates and exports the operator's own monthly summary reports.

We do not request or use Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Photos or any other Google service. We do not read files outside the designated finance folders, and we do not browse the Drive account.

2. How the data is used

  • Extraction. Each document is read to produce a structured record: vendor name, tax ID, document number, date, line items with quantities and prices, VAT, total and payment method.
  • Validation. The record is checked arithmetically (subtotal + VAT = total; quantity × unit price = line total) and against a local database to prevent duplicate entries.
  • Recording. The validated record is appended to the operator's ledger spreadsheet.
  • Filing. The original document is renamed to a descriptive filename and moved to a processed or review folder in the same Drive account.
  • Reporting. Daily and monthly summaries are generated for the operator.

The application performs no advertising, profiling, marketing or scoring, and makes no automated decisions about any individual.

3. Storage and retention

  • Google Drive / Sheets / Docs: documents and ledger rows remain in the operator's own Google account under the operator's control, subject to Google's terms and retention settings.
  • Private server: the application stores extracted records in a local SQLite database, and keeps a working copy of a document only while it is being processed. Local copies are pruned automatically within 24 hours.
  • OAuth credentials: access and refresh tokens are stored in a permission-restricted file (0600) on the private server, used only to call the Google APIs on the operator's behalf. They are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's token endpoint.
  • Retention: extracted records are retained for accounting and tax purposes for the period Thai revenue law requires (generally five years). Anything else can be deleted on request.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. Two categories of processors are used, strictly to deliver the function:

  • Google LLC — Drive, Sheets and Docs APIs, as described above.
  • AI inference providers — the image of a purchase document, and the text extracted from it, are sent to a large-language-model inference API so the document can be read. These requests contain only the document being processed and the instructions for reading it. They are not used to build a profile of the operator, and no Google account credentials, tokens or unrelated file contents are ever included.

We may disclose data if legally compelled by a competent authority under Thai law.

5. Security

  • Access to the private server is restricted to the operator; tokens are stored with owner-only file permissions and are never logged.
  • Transport to Google and to the inference provider uses TLS.
  • The application is single-tenant: one organisation, one Google account, no multi-user access model to breach.

6. Your choices, and how to revoke access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately stops all further access; the application will not be able to read or write any Drive, Sheets or Docs content from that moment.

To request deletion of the records the application derived from your data — the local SQLite entries, the ledger rows, the generated reports, or all of them — write to athikit.supab@gmail.com. Requests are actioned within 30 days; ledger rows that must be kept for statutory accounting purposes will be identified in the reply rather than silently retained.

7. Children

The application is a business accounting tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. Because this is a single-tenant internal tool, changes are also communicated directly to the operator.
