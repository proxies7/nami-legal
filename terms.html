3. No professional advice; verify your books

Document reading is performed by machine. Extracted figures are validated arithmetically and doubtful documents are flagged for human confirmation, but the output is a draft ledger, not an audited record. The operator remains responsible for reviewing entries and for the accuracy of statutory filings. The application does not provide tax, legal or accounting advice.

4. Availability

The application depends on Google APIs and on third-party AI inference services. It may be unavailable during outages, quota limits or credential expiry. Documents are never deleted to work around a failure: unprocessed files remain in the Drive inbox and are picked up on the next run.
