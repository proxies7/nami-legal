Automated purchase-bill bookkeeping

A private automation tool used by a small hospitality and restaurant group in Phuket, Thailand.

What the application does

Nami Accounting watches a single Google Drive folder for newly uploaded purchase bills and invoices (Thai ใบกำกับภาษี, บิลเงินสด, delivery notes and payment slips). For each document it:

  • downloads the file and reads it with an OCR / vision model to extract the vendor, tax ID, document number, date, line items, VAT and totals;
  • validates the arithmetic and checks the entry against a local de-duplication database;
  • appends the structured record to a Google Sheets ledger and moves the original file into an organised folder tree in the same Drive account;
  • sends a summary to the operator, and asks for confirmation when a document is handwritten or incomplete.

Google APIs used

APIScopePurpose
Google DrivedriveList, download, rename and move the purchase documents the operator uploads
Google SheetsspreadsheetsAppend transaction and line-item rows to the operator's own ledger spreadsheet
Google DocsdocumentsExport monthly reports as documents

Nothing else is requested. The application never asks for Gmail, Calendar or Contacts access.

Who can use it

The tool runs on a private server for a single organisation and its own Google account. It is not a public service and does not accept new users. It does not share data with advertisers and holds no third-party analytics.

Your data

Documents stay in the operator's own Google Drive account. Structured records stay in the operator's own Google Sheet and in a local SQLite database on the private server. Document images are sent to an AI inference provider solely to read the text — details in the Privacy Policy.
